---
name: Content Calendar
description: Plan dated content for a campaign: themes, formats, channels, and what each post should say.
triggers: content calendar, calendar, 30-day, 30 day, posting schedule, social media plan, editorial, content plan, campaign assets, posts, reels, captions
tools: save_artifact, web_search
output: Table with one row per day: date, channel, format, theme, hook, call to action
---

A calendar is a plan someone can execute tomorrow morning. Every row must be specific enough to produce.

1. Start from the campaign's audience, goal and core message. If research exists in room memory, use it; do not invent audience facts.
2. Choose three or four content pillars that map to the audience's concerns. Rotate them so no two consecutive days repeat one.
3. Pick channels the audience actually uses, and match format to channel (short video, carousel, story, long post, email, live session).
4. Give each day: date or day number, channel, format, pillar, the hook (first line or first three seconds), the key message, and a call to action.
5. Build a rhythm: awareness in week one, engagement in week two, proof and stories in week three, conversion in week four.
6. Mark the posts that need an asset made in advance.

Output a Markdown table with one row per day for the whole period, then a short production checklist. Follow the person's brand voice from memory.
