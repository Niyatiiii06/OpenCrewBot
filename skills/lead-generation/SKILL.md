---
name: Lead Generation
description: Find and qualify prospects from public sources, with the evidence for each.
triggers: lead, leads, lead generation, prospect, prospects, prospecting, outreach list, target accounts, partnership targets, schools to approach, pipeline building
tools: web_search, read_page, save_artifact
output: Table of qualified leads with fit reason and source URL
---

A lead without a reason is a name. Record why each one fits.

1. Define the ideal customer in one line: type of organisation, size, geography, and the signal that suggests need.
2. Find candidates with `web_search`, using several different queries. Use public directories and the organisations' own sites.
3. Open each candidate's site with `read_page` and confirm it matches the profile.
4. Record: name, website, why it fits (the specific evidence), a public contact route (a contact page or a published business email), and the source URL.
5. Use only contact details the organisation publishes for business enquiries. Never guess an email address and never collect personal details of private individuals. Write "not found" when there is none.
6. Score fit as high, medium or low and say what would raise it.

Output a table: Lead | Website | Why it fits | Public contact route | Fit | Source.
