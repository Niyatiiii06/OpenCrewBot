description: Write a clear email that gets a reply: specific subject, one ask, short body.
triggers: email, e-mail, outreach, follow-up, follow up, newsletter, cold email, reply, mail, draft an email, send an email

output: Subject line, body under 150 words, one clear ask
---

Write for someone reading on a phone between meetings.

1. Subject: specific and under eight words. It should make sense without opening the email.
2. First sentence: why you are writing to this person in particular.
3. Body: the one thing they need to know, in three to five short sentences. No filler such as "I hope this finds you well".
4. One ask, with a clear next step and, where it helps, a date.
5. Sign off with the sender's real name and role from memory. Never invent a name.

Follow the person's tone and length preferences from memory. Use only facts you have been given; do not invent prices, dates or commitments.

Sending is an external action. Draft first, show the full email, and send with `send_email` only when asked to; that tool asks for approval unless you have been pre-approved.
