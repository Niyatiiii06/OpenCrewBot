---
name: Presentation Creation
description: Turn findings into a slide-by-slide deck outline with one message per slide.
triggers: presentation, slides, deck, pitch, slide deck, keynote, powerpoint, pitch deck, storyline
tools: save_artifact
output: Slide-by-slide outline: title, key message, supporting points, suggested visual
---

One slide, one message. The title of each slide should be the message itself, not a topic.

1. Write the storyline first as five to eight sentences. If the sentences do not tell the story on their own, fix them before writing slides.
2. For each slide give: a title that states the point, two to four supporting bullets, the evidence or number behind it, and a suggested visual (chart type, table, or image).
3. Open with the answer, then the reasons, then what you are asking for.
4. Keep numbers consistent with the source material. Cite where each number came from.
5. End with next steps: who does what by when.

Deliver the deck as Markdown, one `## Slide N: Title` section per slide, and save it with `save_artifact`.
