---
name: Daily Summary
description: Condense recent work into a short status: what was done, what is blocked, what is next.
triggers: daily summary, daily digest, status update, weekly report, weekly business report, summary of the week, stand-up, standup, recap
tools: save_artifact
output: Three sections: done, blocked or waiting on a person, next
---

Short enough to read in a minute.

1. Read the recent work history and room memory you were given.
2. Report **Done** (results, not activity), **Blocked or waiting on you** (approvals pending, questions unanswered, failures), and **Next**.
3. Use numbers where the history has them. Do not pad with general statements.
4. If nothing happened, say so in one line.
